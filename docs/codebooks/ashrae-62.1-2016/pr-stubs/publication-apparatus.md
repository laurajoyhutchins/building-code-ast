# ASHRAE 62.1-2016 publication apparatus

Status: draft PR scaffold; implementation not started.

## Depends on

`ashrae-62.1-2016/whole-document-hierarchy`

## Purpose

Classify non-body publication regions and preserve their source roles without flattening them into the numbered standard.

## Scope

- front matter and table of contents
- informative foreword
- normative standard body
- normative appendices A-B
- informative appendices C-K
- continuous-maintenance forms and back matter
- explicit diagnostics for publication apparatus outside the numbered hierarchy

## Boundaries

Do not promote informative apparatus into normative requirements or treat back matter as ordinary provisions.

## Completion gate

Every publication region in the retained artifact is assigned a source role or explicit unresolved diagnostic. Remove this scaffold when implementation replaces it.
