# ACI 318-19 equation semantics

Predecessor: `feature/aci-318-19-definition-graph`.

Owns:
- the reviewed boundary from structural equation regions to faithful glyph/token representation and mathematical expression structure where justified;
- source-role-aware symbol bindings, superscript/subscript identity, scoped definitions, units, and source display form;
- explicit unsupported mathematical notation and confidence/review state;
- separation of published equations, parsed expressions, and reviewed engineering formulas.

Does not own:
- automatic equation execution from raw extraction;
- silent unit conversion;
- project calculations or reinforced-concrete design;
- promotion of commentary derivations into normative formulas.

Completion:
- selected difficult normative and commentary equation families have reviewed expression/symbol/unit representations;
- every normalized representation retains conversion/provenance evidence;
- unsupported notation is explicit;
- no equation is treated as executable merely because it parsed.

Successor: `feature/aci-318-19-table-semantics`.
