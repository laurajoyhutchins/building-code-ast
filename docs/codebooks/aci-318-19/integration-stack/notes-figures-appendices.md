# ACI 318-19 notes, figures, and appendices

Predecessor: `feature/aci-318-19-definition-reference-structure`.

Owns:
- publication-defined notes and their source roles;
- figure and graphical-region boundaries, captions, locators, and exact provenance;
- appendix hierarchy and appendix source-role designation from exact artifact evidence;
- explicit unsupported diagnostics for graphical meaning, ambiguous note roles, and appendix edge cases.

Does not own:
- engineering interpretation of figures;
- semantic meaning inferred from arrows, dimensions, or linework;
- appendix semantics beyond faithful source structure;
- Provision AST interpretation.

Completion:
- representative notes, figures, graphical regions, and appendix structures are preserved or explicitly unsupported;
- commentary figures cannot become normative requirements;
- appendix role is evidence-backed rather than inferred from location;
- generic Document AST validation remains authoritative.

Successor: `feature/aci-318-19-complete-document-ast`.
