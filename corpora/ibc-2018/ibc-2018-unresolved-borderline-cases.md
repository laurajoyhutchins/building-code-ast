# IBC 2018 unresolved and disputed records

## Vector-only uncaptioned graphics

The source contains vector paths used for tables, borders, publisher furniture, and technical drawings. The current normalized diagram inventory accepts two raster technical-graphic candidates. A whole-document vector-region classifier is still needed before vector-only drawings can be counted defensibly. The absence of a normalized record is not an assertion that no such graphics exist.

## Incidental layouts

Twelve regions satisfy the broad geometry policy and four satisfy the stricter recurring-column policy. Every broad-only region remains provisional. Review must confirm that aligned fragments express a data relationship rather than local typography.

## External-reference normalization

Six hundred thirty-four lexical citation occurrences do not currently match a Chapter 35 family. These may include shorthand, title-only references, malformed text-layer output, related codes, federal regulations, or genuine unresolved documents. No unmatched occurrence is silently assigned to a family.

## Internal references

There are 1,314 unresolved, 93 ambiguous, and 424 currently nonexistent-target internal-reference records. Causes include ranges, shorthand, external-standard context, malformed glyph extraction, and targets not represented by the current locator oracle. Resolution must preserve the raw citation string.

## Semantic classifications

Table and figure classes are provisional lexical or chapter-based interpretations. `unknown` is valid. A reviewer may add multiple candidates, dispute a class, or reject it without changing the source record.
